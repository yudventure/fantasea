## Ringkas Inggris

Quiet hideaway of three bungalows in a shaded cove at the entrance to Kabui Bay, where dolphins, turtles and dugongs are often seen.

## Ringkas Indonesia

Tempat menyepi dengan tiga bungalow di teluk teduh di gerbang Teluk Kabui, tempat lumba-lumba, penyu, dan duyung sering terlihat.

## Deskripsi Inggris

Yenwar Homestay occupies a shaded cove on the eastern shore of Gam, right at the entrance to Kabui Bay. Its three bungalows share the sheltered bay with one neighbouring homestay, so the place stays calm and quiet.

There is one bungalow on land with its own bathroom, and two newer private bungalows over the water that use a bathroom building on land. The snorkelling around the homestay is great, and dolphins, turtles and dugongs are often seen in the bay.

Meals are served in the beachside dining shelter, where drinking water, tea and coffee are always free. The limestone islands and channels of Kabui Bay are close by, and the family can take you on snorkelling and sightseeing trips.

## Deskripsi Indonesia

Yenwar Homestay berada di teluk teduh di pantai timur Pulau Gam, tepat di gerbang Teluk Kabui. Tiga bungalownya berbagi teluk yang terlindung ini dengan satu homestay tetangga, jadi suasananya tetap tenang.

Ada satu bungalow di darat dengan kamar mandi dalam, dan dua bungalow pribadi yang lebih baru di atas air yang memakai bangunan kamar mandi di darat. Snorkeling di sekitar homestay sangat bagus, dan lumba-lumba, penyu, serta duyung sering terlihat di teluk.

Makanan disajikan di pondok makan tepi pantai, tempat air minum, teh, dan kopi selalu gratis. Pulau-pulau kapur dan alur Teluk Kabui ada di dekat sini, dan keluarga pemilik bisa mengantar trip snorkeling dan wisata.

## Cara ke sana Inggris

Fly or take the fast ferry from Sorong to Waisai. The homestay picks you up at Waisai by longboat for up to four passengers, and the boat cost is shared by the passengers. Ask us for the current transfer price when you send your request.

## Cara ke sana Indonesia

Naik pesawat atau kapal cepat dari Sorong ke Waisai. Homestay menjemput di Waisai dengan longboat untuk paling banyak empat penumpang, dan biaya perahunya dibagi penumpang. Tanyakan harga antar jemput terbaru saat mengirim permintaan.

## Perlu diketahui Inggris

- The phone signal is patchy, so do not count on a steady internet connection
- Friwen and Yenbeser villages are about 15 minutes away by boat for supplies and a stronger 4G signal
- Please take plastic and other inorganic rubbish home with you

## Perlu diketahui Indonesia

- Sinyal telepon naik turun, jadi jangan mengandalkan internet yang stabil
- Kampung Friwen dan Yenbeser sekitar 15 menit dengan perahu untuk membeli kebutuhan dan sinyal 4G yang lebih kuat
- Tolong bawa pulang sampah plastik dan sampah anorganik lainnya
