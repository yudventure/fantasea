## Ringkas Inggris

Dive homestay with over-water and beach bungalows on a white sand bay at Kordiris, next to the Dore Dor mangroves, with its own dive centre and great birdwatching.

## Ringkas Indonesia

Homestay selam dengan bungalow di atas air dan di pantai, di teluk berpasir putih Kordiris dekat hutan bakau Dore Dor, dengan pusat selam sendiri dan tempat pengamatan burung yang kaya.

## Deskripsi Inggris

Kordiris Dive Homestay sits in the shelter of a point at the western end of a perfect white sand bay. It is on the islet of Kordiris, which is separated from the Gam mainland by the Dore Dor mangrove waterway.

The homestay has its own dive centre and takes guests to the popular sites of the Dampier Strait. Back home, the sandy beach leads to seagrass and reef where dugongs are sometimes seen and walking sharks are regular visitors. From April to May whales can be spotted offshore as they move through the strait.

The winding mangrove channels and the jungle of Dore Dor are a paradise for birdwatchers, with hornbills, sea eagles, Eclectus parrots, kingfishers and herons. The forest behind the homestay is home to cuscus, black king cockatoos and bush fowl that build huge nest mounds.

Meals are served in the beachside dining room, where drinking water, tea and coffee are always available.

## Deskripsi Indonesia

Kordiris Dive Homestay berada di balik tanjung di ujung barat sebuah teluk berpasir putih. Letaknya di Pulau Kordiris, pulau kecil yang dipisahkan dari daratan Gam oleh alur bakau Dore Dor.

Homestay ini punya pusat selam sendiri dan mengantar tamu ke titik selam populer di Selat Dampier. Di depan homestay, pantai berpasir berlanjut ke padang lamun dan karang, tempat duyung kadang terlihat dan hiu berjalan sering datang. Pada April sampai Mei paus bisa terlihat di lepas pantai saat melintasi selat.

Alur bakau yang berkelok dan hutan Dore Dor adalah surga pengamat burung, dengan rangkong, elang laut, nuri bayan, raja udang, dan kuntul. Hutan di belakang homestay menjadi rumah kuskus, kakatua raja hitam, dan burung gosong yang membangun gundukan sarang besar.

Makanan disajikan di ruang makan tepi pantai, tempat air minum, teh, dan kopi selalu tersedia.

## Cara ke sana Inggris

Fly or take the fast ferry from Sorong to Waisai. The homestay picks you up at Waisai by speedboat, which carries up to four passengers, and the boat cost is shared by the passengers. Ask us for the current transfer price when you send your request.

## Cara ke sana Indonesia

Naik pesawat atau kapal cepat dari Sorong ke Waisai. Homestay menjemput di Waisai dengan speedboat untuk paling banyak empat penumpang, dan biaya perahunya dibagi penumpang. Tanyakan harga antar jemput terbaru saat mengirim permintaan.

- This is a busy homestay, so book early in high season
