## Ringkas Inggris

Four quiet beach bungalows in a secluded cove on Kordiris, off southern Gam, with healthy coral at the door and the Dore Dor mangroves behind.

## Ringkas Indonesia

Empat bungalow pantai yang tenang di teluk tersembunyi di Pulau Kordiris, selatan Gam, dengan karang sehat di depan pintu dan hutan bakau Dore Dor di belakangnya.

## Deskripsi Inggris

Wainay Homestay sits on a thin arc of secluded beach in a quiet cove off the southern coast of Gam. It is on the islet of Kordiris, separated from Gam by the Dore Dor mangrove waterway, a place full of birds.

The four bungalows stand on the beach, and each verandah has wide ocean views, a table and chairs and a hammock. The snorkelling over healthy coral is excellent, dolphins and turtles visit often, and whales can be seen offshore in April and May.

Meals are served in the beachside dining shelter, a sand-floored space with a big table and benches. Drinking water, tea and coffee are always free. The family can take you sightseeing anywhere you like, all the way to Wayag.

## Deskripsi Indonesia

Wainay Homestay berada di lengkung pantai yang tersembunyi di teluk tenang di pantai selatan Gam. Letaknya di Pulau Kordiris, yang dipisahkan dari Gam oleh alur bakau Dore Dor yang penuh burung.

Empat bungalownya berdiri di pantai, dan setiap beranda punya pemandangan laut yang luas, meja dan kursi, serta hammock. Snorkeling di atas karang yang sehat sangat bagus, lumba-lumba dan penyu sering datang, dan paus bisa terlihat di lepas pantai pada April dan Mei.

Makanan disajikan di pondok makan tepi pantai berlantai pasir dengan meja besar dan bangku panjang. Air minum, teh, dan kopi selalu gratis. Keluarga pemilik bisa mengantar wisata ke mana saja, sampai ke Wayag.

## Kegiatan Inggris

- Snorkelling over healthy coral in front of the homestay
- Looking for dolphins and turtles, and whales offshore in April and May
- Birdwatching in the Dore Dor mangroves, with hornbills, sea eagles, Eclectus parrots, kingfishers and herons
- Looking for red birds of paradise, cuscus and black king cockatoos in the forest behind the homestay
- Sightseeing trips anywhere in Raja Ampat, including Wayag

## Kegiatan Indonesia

- Snorkeling di atas karang sehat di depan homestay
- Mencari lumba-lumba dan penyu, juga paus di lepas pantai pada April dan Mei
- Mengamati burung di hutan bakau Dore Dor, ada rangkong, elang laut, nuri bayan, raja udang, dan kuntul
- Mencari cenderawasih merah, kuskus, dan kakatua raja hitam di hutan belakang homestay
- Trip wisata ke mana saja di Raja Ampat, termasuk Wayag

## Cara ke sana Inggris

Fly or take the fast ferry from Sorong to Waisai. The homestay picks you up at Waisai by a small longboat that carries two passengers, and the boat cost is shared by the passengers. Ask us for the current transfer price when you send your request.

## Cara ke sana Indonesia

Naik pesawat atau kapal cepat dari Sorong ke Waisai. Homestay menjemput di Waisai dengan longboat kecil untuk dua penumpang, dan biaya perahunya dibagi penumpang. Tanyakan harga antar jemput terbaru saat mengirim permintaan.

## Perlu diketahui Inggris

- Bring your own fins if you need a large size
- Only a little English is spoken, so a translation app or phrasebook helps
- Friwen and Yenbeser villages are about 15 minutes away by boat for supplies and a stronger signal
- Please take plastic and other inorganic rubbish home with you

## Perlu diketahui Indonesia

- Bawa fin sendiri kalau butuh ukuran besar
