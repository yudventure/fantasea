## Ringkas Inggris

Homestay in Misool near Dapunlol, an area known for its viewpoint over the karst islands and for ancient rock art on the limestone cliffs.

## Ringkas Indonesia

Homestay di Misool dekat Dapunlol, kawasan yang dikenal dengan bukit pandang di atas pulau karst dan lukisan batu kuno di tebing kapur.

## Deskripsi Inggris

Dapunlol Homestay takes its name from Dapunlol in Misool, the large island group in the far south of Raja Ampat, about 150 km by boat southwest of Sorong.

The Dapunlol area is known for the Harfat Jaya viewpoint, where a climb opens a wide view over the karst islets, and for ancient rock paintings on the limestone walls along the water. Kayak and boat trips through Misool often stop at the Dapunlol jetty and peak.

## Deskripsi Indonesia

Dapunlol Homestay dinamai dari Dapunlol di Misool, gugusan pulau besar di ujung selatan Raja Ampat, sekitar 150 km dengan perahu ke arah barat daya Sorong.

Kawasan Dapunlol dikenal dengan bukit pandang Harfat Jaya, tempat pendakian membuka pemandangan luas ke pulau-pulau karst, dan dengan lukisan batu kuno di dinding kapur di tepi laut. Trip kayak dan perahu di Misool sering singgah di dermaga dan puncak Dapunlol.
